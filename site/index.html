The hard part starts after the invitation.

A guest can get into a wedding site and still leave the couple with a difficult job: managing the replies.

That is the starting problem for this proposal. We want to make it easy to see who is invited, who has answered, and what each answer means for the weekend.

One organizer’s experience started this work. The broader needs below are proposals to test, not a claim that every couple needs a new tool.

What runs behind the scenes?

The planned application uses SvelteKit on Cloudflare Workers, with Supabase for sign-in, the database, and private image storage. Content and style editing belong inside the product; a separate CMS is not planned.

The service operator handles hosting and technical setup. The couple handles events, people, invitations, replies, and presentation. This walkthrough itself is a static Cloudflare Pages site.

Guest entry, invitation delivery, reply permissions, and rules for changing already-invited events still need agreement. The production application has not been built.
