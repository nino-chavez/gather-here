# Gather Here design

This presentation extends the selected wedding prototype’s paper, ink, and cobalt system. It does not introduce a new visual identity.

Paper #f6f5f1; panel #ffffff; ink #17213b; link/action #1545c6; line #d6d8dc; focus #0a66ff. Guest cover #f7f1e6; alternate blue cover #365b70. Georgia/Times serif for display; system sans for instructions. Pinyon Script is reserved for fictional couple names.

Use flat editorial sections, clear rules, generous separation between topics, and readable body measure. Controls have at least 44px targets. Preserve real prototype behavior and labels. Mobile order follows the narrative; no horizontal page overflow. No decorative metrics, generic icon cards, or invented customer outcomes.

## Surfaces

- Walkthrough: an organizer decides whether this product solves the RSVP-management problem. Explain the plan, show the product, guide real prototype tasks, and collect discussion prompts without a submission form.
- Prototype: preserve the selected organizer, guest, and style-editor experiences. Add an explicit return path and public-demo notice.

Character: calm, personal, practical, precise, welcoming. Avoid corporate dashboards, wedding ornament, consultant language, and overstated readiness. Motion is unnecessary for reading and trying the product.

## Preserve

Keep prototype record identity, shared attendance questions, permission simulations, error recovery, curated styles, local persistence, and keyboard controls. Keep the distinction between proposed requirements and demonstrated behavior.
